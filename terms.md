# Wiseworm Terms of Use

**Effective date: October 26, 2026**

These terms explain how you may use Wiseworm, an Android app for organizing and revisiting book highlights and notes. By using the app, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

## Using Wiseworm

Wiseworm supports compatible Kindle notes PDFs from your device and compatible Google Play Books notes documents selected through a connected Google Drive account. It imports notes and highlights, not complete books. Document layouts and formats can vary, so an import may be incomplete or unsuccessful. Check imported passages before relying on them.

You are responsible for the content you import, add, export, or share and for using it in accordance with applicable rights and rules. Wiseworm does not grant rights to book content owned by others.

## Optional Google services

Google Drive access is optional, except when you want to import Google Play Books notes through the supported connection or use Drive features. You choose whether to authorize access, select documents, enable automatic updates, and upload backups. You can disconnect the account or revoke access through your Google Account settings.

Gemini island naming is separate and optional. It requires your own API key and an internet connection. When you use it, samples of your highlights are sent to Gemini. Google's terms, policies, availability, and any applicable usage limits or charges for your key also apply. Keep the key private.

## Your data and backups

Your library is stored on your device. You are responsible for keeping a backup if you want to preserve it when switching devices, clearing app data, or uninstalling. Restoring a backup replaces the app's current data; export it first if you need to retain it. Google Drive backup is an optional additional method. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for details about data handling.

## App results and availability

Semantic search, map positions, island groupings, and suggested names are aids to exploring your reading, not definitive classifications. Review any result that matters to you. Features may depend on device resources, compatible source documents, an internet connection, or the availability of Google services. The app may change as it develops.

## Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version; the updated terms will be published here. For questions or to report an issue, use the [Wiseworm releases repository's Issues tab](https://github.com/GimmeCheez/wiseworm-releases/issues). Do not include private highlights or credentials in a public issue.
