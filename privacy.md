# Wiseworm Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 26, 2026**

This policy describes how Wiseworm handles information when you use the Android app. Wiseworm is a reading highlights and notes app. Google Drive and Gemini are optional services with separate purposes.

## Information Wiseworm handles

The app stores the books, highlights, notes, edits, review activity, and related information you add or import in its local app data on your device. It processes highlights on your device to support semantic search and the map. The app may also store the settings and credentials needed for optional connections you enable. An exported backup contains your library and activity history, so store backup files somewhere you trust.

## Google Drive access

If you connect your Google account and authorize Google Drive access, Wiseworm uses that access to let you choose compatible notes documents, import their highlights and notes, and check those selected documents for updates when you request a check or enable automatic updates. Google Play Books notes are imported through this connection; downloading those documents as DOCX or PDF files and importing them from device storage is not supported.

If you choose Google Drive backups, Wiseworm also uses the authorized connection to upload a backup and access it for restoration. A backup contains your Wiseworm library and activity history. The app may keep the information needed to identify selected documents and show synchronization or backup status. Changes you make inside Wiseworm are not written back to the original notes documents. Deleting a source document from Drive does not automatically delete highlights already imported into the app.

You can use the app's core library and review features without connecting Google Drive. If you disconnect your Google account or revoke Wiseworm's access in your Google Account settings, Drive import, updates, and backups will no longer work until you reconnect. Disconnecting does not by itself remove information already imported into your local library or erase backups stored in Drive; delete those separately if you wish.

## Optional Gemini naming

If you set up a personal Gemini API key and use automatic island naming, Wiseworm sends samples of your highlights to Google's Gemini service to suggest names for related groups of passages. This requires an internet connection. You can choose to name islands yourself instead and remove your key in Settings. Google's handling of the data sent to Gemini is subject to the terms and privacy information associated with your Gemini account and API use.

## Storage, sharing, and your choices

Your library is stored on your device. Wiseworm sends data to Google Drive for the Drive features you enable and sends highlight samples to Gemini when you use automatic naming. Files you export or share are handled by the destination you choose. Wiseworm does not need a Google connection for local Kindle PDF import, browsing and editing highlights, reviews, semantic search, or map exploration after local processing.

You can edit or delete highlights and notes in the app, remove your Gemini key, disconnect Google Drive, export a backup, or delete the app's local data through Android. Before uninstalling the app or clearing its data, export a current backup if you want to keep your library. You can manage Google Drive backups in your Drive account and revoke app access in your Google Account settings. Removing local data does not automatically delete copies that you previously exported or uploaded.

## Security and updates

Keep your Google account credentials, Gemini API key, and backup files private. This policy may change when Wiseworm's features or data handling change. The effective date above identifies the current version; the updated policy will be published on this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, [open an issue in the Wiseworm releases repository](https://github.com/GimmeCheez/wiseworm-releases/issues). Do not post access keys, private highlights, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
