# Wiseworm

**Rediscover what you've read. Connect ideas. Keep knowledge close.**

Wiseworm is an Android app that brings your book highlights and notes into one library. Search passages by keyword or meaning, explore related ideas on a map, and revisit what you have read through spaced reviews. It runs on Android 14 or later and is available in English, Brazilian Portuguese, and Spanish.

## What Wiseworm does

- Import a Kindle PDF of exported notes and highlights from your device. Connect Google Drive to select compatible Google Play Books notes documents. Wiseworm imports notes, not entire books.
- Browse, edit, filter, and add highlights in one library.
- Search by meaning and explore islands of related highlights on an interactive map. Processing, semantic search, and map exploration run on your device.
- Review highlights at your own pace and track your reading activity.
- Export and restore a backup. You can optionally connect Google Drive for document updates and backups.
- Optionally connect Gemini with your own API key to suggest island names. When enabled, this feature sends samples of your highlights to Gemini.

You can use Kindle import, your library, reviews, semantic search, and the map without connecting a Google account or Gemini. Google Play Books notes import and Google Drive features require a Google account connection. Gemini naming requires an internet connection and a separate setup.

## Get started

Download the current Android APK from the [Wiseworm releases](https://github.com/GimmeCheez/wiseworm-releases/releases) page. Import a compatible notes file in the app's Settings, then let Wiseworm prepare your highlights for semantic search and the map. The [project README](https://github.com/GimmeCheez/wiseworm-releases#readme) has more details about the app and its features.

## Your data

Your Wiseworm library is stored on your device. You control whether to connect Google Drive, upload a backup, or use Gemini to generate island names. Read the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for details about these choices and Google user data.
