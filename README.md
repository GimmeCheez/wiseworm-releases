# Wiseworm

**Rediscover what you've read. Connect ideas. Keep knowledge close.**

Wiseworm brings your book highlights and notes together in one Android app. Organize your reading, find passages by meaning, discover connections across topics, and revisit ideas over time.

Instead of leaving your highlights scattered across books and files, build a library you can search, explore, and review.

## What you can do

### Bring your highlights together

Import your Kindle and Google Play Books notes. Browse passages from different books in one place, keep your notes alongside them, and manually add new highlights to books in your library.

| Source | Supported import |
| :--- | :--- |
| Kindle | A PDF exported from your notes and highlights. |
| Google Play Books | Notes documents accessed through your connected Google Drive account. |
| Google Drive | Compatible notes documents that you choose to import. |

Import your **notes and highlights**, not the full book. PDFs and documents that use other layouts or contain other types of content may not be recognized.

### Organize your library

Search your highlights, filter by book and color, edit passages and notes, and hide anything you don't want to review. You can also delete content and edit book titles.

When you import a file from the same book again, the app recognizes existing highlights to help avoid duplicates.

### Search by meaning

Find an idea even when you can't remember the exact wording. Try entering a topic, a question, or a description of what you're looking for.

For example: “How do institutions influence a country's development?”

Search by meaning complements keyword search and runs on your device once your highlights are ready.

### Explore your map of ideas

See your highlights on an interactive map. Each point represents a highlight, and its color identifies the book it comes from. Islands bring together passages about related topics, including passages from different books.

Zoom in for more detail, drag to move around, and use filters to focus on specific books or islands. As you zoom in, small cards can show the book title and part of the passage.

The map's visual markers help you find your way:

- **Gray lines:** connect highlights with similar ideas from the same book.
- **Colored lines:** connect similar highlights from different books, helping you discover connections across your reading. Their colors blend the colors of the books involved.
- **A thin ring around a highlight:** marks a bridge between different islands and books. These points are useful starting places for exploring connections across topics.
- **A small dot at the center of a highlight, next to an island's name:** marks the highlight that serves as a reference point for that island's topic.
- **A larger, thicker ring:** marks the highlight you've selected. Its connections are also emphasized.
- **Thicker lines with numbers:** show your exploration trail and the order of the highlights you've visited. They trace your route; they don't necessarily indicate a stronger similarity between passages.

Some markers appear only when you zoom in or select a highlight. Use the display controls to show or hide lines and labels.

You can give islands your own names. Automatic naming with Gemini is optional.

The map is a way to explore your library. A passage's position and an island's name are suggestions for organizing your reading, rather than definitive classifications.

### Revisit ideas over time

Wiseworm spreads out your reviews so you can return to what you've read without going through your entire library at once.

During a review, choose:

- **Show more:** to see that content more often.
- **Show less:** to leave more time between future reviews of that content.

Choose based on how often you'd like to revisit the idea. These buttons adjust review frequency; they don't delete the highlight.

### Track your reading habits

See the days you've studied, your review streak, and how your highlights are distributed across books. You can also track your **reading diversity**, which reflects how evenly your highlights are spread across your books, and your **interdisciplinary regions**, which bring together contributions from different books around related topics.

Explore how your reading connects, keep track of your activity, and build a routine at your own pace.

### Bring in new notes from Google Drive

Connect your Google account and choose the notes documents you want to keep up with. Check for updates manually or turn on automatic updates.

This connection brings notes from those documents into Wiseworm. Changes you make in the app are not sent back to the original documents.

## Getting started

### 1. Install and open the app

Install the Android installation file (APK) provided by the project. If Android asks you to allow installation from that source, follow the instructions on your device.

You'll need **Android 8.0 or later**. You can start with local Kindle files without connecting Google Drive or setting up Gemini.

### 2. Import your first book

Open **Settings** and find the import section. The steps depend on where your highlights come from:

- **Kindle:** select the PDF of notes and highlights exported from Kindle and saved on your device.
- **Google Play Books:** connect your Google Drive account, grant the requested access, and choose the notes documents you want to import.

**Google Play Books notes can only be imported through the Google Drive connection.** Downloading those documents as DOCX or PDF files and importing them from your device is not supported.

When the import is complete, check the import report and open your **Library** to look through the passages. Starting with one book makes it easier to get familiar with the app before bringing in your whole collection.

### 3. Let the app prepare your highlights

After importing, Wiseworm prepares your highlights for search by meaning and the map. This may take some time, especially during your first import or with a larger library.

You can browse your passages in the Library while this is happening. If search by meaning or the map isn't available yet, wait for the processing shown in the app to finish.

### 4. Explore and try your first review

Open the Map to see how your topics are arranged. Use zoom and filters to explore your books, then select a highlight you'd like to read again.

On the Review screen, read the passages and choose **Show more** or **Show less**. Come back on other days for your next reviews.

### 5. Set up a backup

In Settings, export a backup and save it somewhere outside the app's own storage. If you use Google Drive, you can also turn on automatic backups and grant the requested permission. We recommend keeping automatic backups enabled, since your library is stored on your device unless you back it up.

Before switching devices, clearing app data, or uninstalling Wiseworm, make sure your backup is up to date.

## Automatic island names (optional)

Wiseworm can use Gemini to suggest names for the topics on your map. To turn it on:

1. Open **Settings** and find the Gemini section.
2. Choose the option to get a key, which opens Google's service.
3. Get your personal access key and return to the app.
4. Paste the key into the field provided and save it.
5. Wait for the names to be generated. If needed, use the option to name your islands again.

Treat your key as a private credential. Don't share it in messages, screenshots, or issue reports. You can remove it in Settings.

This feature requires an internet connection. When you use it, **samples of your highlights are sent to Gemini** to identify topics and suggest names. Check Google's terms and usage limits when setting up the service.

You can still use the Library, reviews, search by meaning, and the map without setting up Gemini. Islands may have temporary names, which you can change yourself.

## Offline use and optional services

| Feature | Internet required? |
| :--- | :--- |
| Browse and edit saved highlights | No. |
| Import a Kindle notes PDF saved on your device | No. |
| Review highlights and view statistics | No. |
| Prepare highlights and search by meaning | No. |
| Explore the map once your highlights are ready | No. |
| Generate names with Gemini | Yes. |
| Import updates from Google Drive and upload backups | Yes. |

Initial processing may use more of your device's resources. Automatic tasks may also wait until Android's connection and battery requirements are met.

## Your data, your choices

Your library is stored on your device. Google Drive and Gemini are optional connections that you choose to enable.

- **Gemini:** receives samples of your highlights when you use automatic naming.
- **Google Drive:** lets you import selected documents and store a backup when backups are enabled.
- **Exported backups:** contain your library and activity history. Keep them somewhere you trust.

Edits made in Wiseworm stay in the app. Importing notes again does not keep every change mirrored between two apps.

## Backup and restore

Use the export option in Settings to create a backup. To recover your data, choose the restore option and select a compatible Wiseworm backup.

**Restoring a backup replaces the app's current data.** If you want to keep that data, export a backup before continuing. After a restore, the app may need to prepare search by meaning and the map again.

Google Drive backups are another option. Check the backup status and last upload date in the app, especially before switching devices.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to connect my Google account?

You don't need a Google account connection to import Kindle PDFs, browse your library, or review highlights. You do need it to import Google Play Books notes and use Google Drive features. Gemini setup is separate and optional.

### Does Wiseworm open entire books?

No. Wiseworm focuses on highlights and notes. Import compatible notes from Kindle or Google Play Books.

### Why is my map still empty?

Your highlights need to be prepared after importing. Wait for processing to finish and make sure you have enough highlights to display. You can browse the imported content in your Library in the meantime.

### Why do some islands still have no name?

Names may appear after the map is ready. Automatic naming depends on your Gemini setup, internet connection, and service availability. Some small groups may also remain without a generated name. You can edit island names yourself.

### Can I import the same file again?

Yes. Wiseworm recognizes highlights already imported from the same book. Importing again can bring in additional passages and update information from the source.

### Do edits I make in the app appear in Kindle or Google Play Books?

No. Changes made in Wiseworm stay in your library within the app.

### Will deleting a document from Google Drive delete my highlights in the app?

Not automatically. Highlights you've already imported stay in your library, where you can manage them in Wiseworm.

### Does the app work on iPhone or Android versions earlier than Android 14?

No. This version is for devices running Android 14 or later.
